Units and interpretation

Particle mode converts charge from nC to C and magnetic field from mT to T; velocity is in m/s, electric field in V/m, and force in N. Wire mode uses current in A, segment length in m, magnetic field in T, and force in N. The wire length vector points in the conventional current direction. The view is a rotatable projection, not a scale-accurate 3D scene.
