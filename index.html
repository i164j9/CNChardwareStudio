How much does a small angle error move a point?

Imagine a point on an arm or shaft that is slightly turned away from where it should be. This estimates how far that point shifts.

Example: a point 100 mm from the center moves about 1.75 mm when the angle changes by 1°. Change the example values below to match your hardware.

How the point moves during that time

For a simple estimate, this assumes the point moves at a steady speed from start to finish. Real machines may speed up and slow down.
