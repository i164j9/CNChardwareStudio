Engineering assumptions

Mechanical shaft output is converted to estimated motor/drive electrical input using the explicit efficiency block. DC supply efficiency is a separate loss. Wire-drop warnings compare calculated drop against the adjustable design threshold; they are diagnostics, not protective interlocks. Three-phase spindle current is an independent balanced-AC estimate unless explicitly connected.
